magic

is a dharmic technology for liberation

A teaching by Vajrabudha  ·  Vajrakula

Opening · Invocation

Namo Guru Avalokitesvaraya!

Buddha of limitless compassion,

You take on all forms needed and use all means necessary to help beings.

Increase our wisdom and compassion so that we may also use all means, material and magical, to do so!

I · Thesis

My thesis

Buddhist magic is an application of wisdom and compassion.

II · A working definition

A working definition

Through impermanence & interdependence

Through merit & wisdom

For the sake of beings

  1. i.

    The ability to produce effects in the world through supernatural means.

    This covers most of our bases — blessings, protections, banishings, divination.

  2. ii.

    through the skilful arrangement of causes and conditions to produce outcomes,

    Karma simply means “causes produce outcomes.”

  3. iii.

    by the dedication of merit and wisdom

    Merit — positive karma that leads to liberation. Wisdom — experiential understanding of emptiness.

  4. iv.

    in order to benefit beings.

     

III · Causes & conditions

The four dharma seals

Impermanence & interdependence

  1. i.All compounded things are impermanent.
  2. ii.Everything influenced by ignorance creates suffering.
  3. iii.All phenomena are without inherent existence.
  4. iv.Nirvana is beyond extremes.
IV · Helping and liberating beings

You meet a shop owner

“My business is suffering!”

Which response is more compassionate?

One

You feel bad because you are attached to money. Let go of your attachment and your suffering will end.

Two

Let me help you bring in business so you can remain financially afloat.

By intervening in causes and conditions to alleviate his material suffering, we help him to practice diligently to alleviate ultimate suffering.

Magic functions as the interplay between relative and ultimate liberation.

V · Magic across the three streams
TheravādaMahāyānaVajrayāna

How do the three streams of Buddhism employ magic to benefit beings?

A non-exhaustive survey. Yes — there is magic in all of them!

Atanatiya Sutta

A protection ritual that invokes the Four Great Kings — protectors of Buddhism and Buddhists — and their spirit armies. A directional protection, to help Buddhists quell disturbances so we can live and practice in peace.

Cundi Dharani Sutra

Dharani sutras rely on the blessings of the buddhas to produce benefits for practitioners. Cundi is a form of Avalokitesvara / Guanyin.

namaḥ saptānāṁ samyak-saṁbuddha koṭināṁ | tad-yathā oṁ cale cule cundi svāhā ||

Out of compassion, Buddhist magic arises.

The four enlightened activities

Each produces relative and ultimate benefit, and each manifests on the outer (material), inner (mental and emotional) and secret (wisdom) levels.

Destroying — destroy my ignorance so I might have merit and wisdom; grant me the resilience and power to face my challenges; get rid of external obstacles.

A noteVajrayana is a strictly initiation-based tradition. These examples are illustrations, not recommendations to practice. Seek out a Vajrayana guru to discuss further.

VI · Two common objections

The objection

“Magic is worldly, not Buddhist.”

Doing magic to improve your material conditions strengthens your attachments.

The response

The view of Buddhist magic is interdependence and compassion.

The objection

“Magic is just culture.”

“Real Buddhism” is a philosophy. Everything magical is a cultural add-on that does not exist in “Real Buddhism”.

The response

How else do the buddhas and their teachings arise if not through culture?

VII · Practical — the Ganapati Hridaya Dharani Sutra

Practical

The Ganapati Hridaya Dharani Sutra

Ganapati is a liberated buddha who can grant both material and spiritual attainments — a form of Avalokitesvara who, out of compassion, specialises in clearing obstacles and increasing wealth, on both the material and spiritual level.

Bonus transmission

OM GAM GANAPATAYE SVAHA

Not part of the sutra — a separate mantra of Ganapati, to build your connection with him.

Magical functions

And above all, as the sutra states — attaining the mind of awakening.

How to work the sutra

  1. i.Take refuge in Ganapati and the Triple Gem
  2. ii.Set your intention — to help beings
  3. iii.Recite the sutra
  4. iv.Within the frame of the sutra, recite the dharani seven times
  5. v.Make your wish
  6. vi.Stomp your foot thrice
VIII · Dedication of merit

Namo Guru Avalokitesvaraya!

Buddha of limitless compassion,

You take on all forms needed and use all means necessary to help beings.

By the merit of this practice, may we all attain your state to help all beings with magic, and to bring all beings beyond it too.

Om swasti!   Sarva mangalam!
