i · The paradox

Buddhism teaches there is no fixed self.

Yet nearly every Buddhist culture built a dense technology of funerals, feeding rites, guidance for the dying, and care for the dead.

Cuevas & Stone (eds.), The Buddhist Dead (2007)

i · The paradox

Three kinds of necromancy

I

Speak with

The dead addressed directly. A lama guiding the deceased through the interval after death.

II

Use

Remains, relics and corpses as instruments. The most contested kind.

III

Serve

Feeding, remembering, releasing. The obligations the living owe.

Buddhism does all three, under very different rules.

iii · Serving the dead

A text spoken to the dead: the Bardo Thödol

Origin

A terma

Revealed by Karma Lingpa, traditionally dated to the fourteenth century.

Its use

A guide, not a spell

Read to guide the dying and the dead through the interval. (Gouin 2010)

Title

A 1927 English name

“The Tibetan Book of the Dead” is a Western cultural object. (Cuevas 2003)

The text is published. The practice is transmitted.
