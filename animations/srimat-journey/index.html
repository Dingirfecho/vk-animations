A Guided Meditation for Vajrakula

Journey to the Land Endowed with Splendor

A visualization of Śrīmat, the southern pure land of Buddha Ratnasambhava
रत्नसम्भव
Before you begin

A place of refuge, not a personal projection.

The destination is fixed, its topography defined by the Ratna family. The motivation is not to escape anything, but to stand in the presence of Ratnasambhava and Māmakī, and let the Wisdom of Equanimity transmute whatever pride, comparison or scarcity you bring with you.
A thirty-minute session that holds even one element clearly is worth far more than a rushed journey through the whole landscape.
Before the journey

Go for refuge.

Buddha, Dharma, Sangha — and the root masters and deities of the field of refuge.

Generate bodhicitta.

Dedicate this practice, and whatever benefit comes of it, to wandering beings without exception.

Closing

Dedicate the merit.

For the complete removal of suffering and poverty for all wandering beings everywhere. You bring nothing back from this land except the quiet certainty of equanimity.

May all beings realize the inexhaustible treasury of their own minds.

Composed and arranged by Guru Prajnavajra, from the format taught and transmitted by Sri Suryagridha for Vajrakula.

Sarva Mangalam!
