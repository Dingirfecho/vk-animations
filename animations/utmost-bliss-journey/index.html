A Guided Meditation for Vajrakula

Journey to the Land of Utmost Bliss

A visualization of Sukhāvatī, the pure land of Amitābha
अमिताभ
Before the journey

Go for refuge.

Buddha, Dharma, Sangha — and your own teachers.

Generate bodhicitta.

Dedicate this practice to the liberation of all beings.

Closing

Dedicate the merit.

To your teachers, to all beings, and to the swift and complete
removal of suffering everywhere.

May all beings find their way to the Purelands.
